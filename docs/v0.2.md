## 0. Executive decision

We are constructing an **interlingua** (a shared intermediate conceptual representation) that sits between descriptions belonging to particular metaphysical traditions and a **compositional visual grammar** (meaning created through rules for combining visual elements).

**Proposed pipeline:**

`Primary text → contextual interpretation → scoped formal claim → conceptual composition → visual rendering → comprehension test`

The system must represent incompatible doctrines *without endorsing any of them*. A named referent in the corpus is **not** an assertion that the referent exists in our world. A tradition's descriptions are treated as statements *within that tradition*. Scholarly citations and interpretive notes live in research data, not as continuously displayed on-screen caveats. A finished video can begin with a single attribution/disclaimer.

**Decision:** retain v0.1's cosmological mechanisms while adding experience, awareness, mental representation, deliberation, agency, moral evaluation, modal claims (possible versus necessary), and logical implication. Use Dennett as a stress test, not an ontological authority.

## 1. Architecture

| Layer | What it does | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Source representation** | Preserve native terminology and location in text | Ouspensky's account of self-remembering |
| **Worldview scope** | Identify which lore or hypothetical model asserts a proposition | `gurdjieff`, `scenario_compatibilism` |
| **Referents** | Declare names and their grammatical types without presupposing actual existence | `person:entity`, `anger:episode` |
| **Atomic relations** | Express what connects those referents | `experiences(person, anger)` |
| **Logical composition** | Express conjunction, negation, conditionals, modalities | `not(can(person, attack))` |
| **Rendering specification** | Transform relations into structure, geometry, motion, texture, iconic figures, and optional conventional symbols | an attention direction and a selected branch |

The **semantic core** consists of proposition composition (`and`, `or`, `not`, `implies`, `exists`, `modal`) and typed atomic relations. Domain vocabulary—e.g. `experiences`, `grounded_in`, or `morally_responsible`—is a replaceable semantic module. We do **not** claim the list of predicates is minimal or philosophically irreducible.

This distinction matters: 39 named predicates are a **working lexicon**, not 39 visual primitives.

## 2. A minimal, typed grammar

```text
expression := atom(predicate, typed_roles, optional_parameters)
            | and(expression, ...)
            | or(expression, ...)
            | not(expression)
            | implies(expression, expression)
            | exists(entity)
            | modal(domain, mode, expression, optional_agent, optional_background)

modal domain := alethic | deontic | epistemic | agential
```

Definitions at a practical level:

- **Alethic modality**: whether something is possible or necessary in a model.
- **Deontic modality**: what is required or permitted by a normative rule, not necessarily what occurs.
- **Epistemic modality**: what is known or believed according to an epistemic standpoint (a perspective on knowledge).
- **Agential modality**: what an agent can do, with background conditions specified where needed.
- **Typed roles**: constraints specifying the expected kinds of arguments, such as a subject and an episode for `experiences`.

A symbol's declared type classifies its **role in an expression**, not its ultimate metaphysical nature. A tradition can posit an incorporeal agent, a conscious universe, or an abstract intelligence without the grammar deciding what is materially real.

### 2.1 Four deliberate separations

**Experience versus awareness.**

`experiences(subject, episode)` means the subject has a particular experiential episode; `aware_of(subject, episode)` means the subject takes that episode as something of which it is aware. `available_for_use(content, subject)` means content can be accessed for thought/action; `attends_to(subject, target)` indicates selective direction of attention. **No entailment between these is hard-coded.** A tradition may add one, such as “all experience requires awareness.”

**Choice versus free will.**

`selects(subject, option)` states a selection. It does not entail `can(subject, alternative_action)`, `agent_causes(subject, action)`, or `morally_responsible(subject, action)`. These remain independent assertions.

**Explanation versus reality.**

`attributes(observer, system, belief_like)` means an observer *interprets* a system as having belief-like states. It does not imply subjective experience actually occurs within the system. This preserves the distinction between Dennett's intentional stance and phenomenal experience.

**Cause versus implication versus moral rule.**

`causes(A,B)`, `implies(A,B)`, and `obligatory(B)` describe distinct things. A logical consequence is not automatically a physical production; being obligated to act does not guarantee the action occurs.

### 2.2 Scope and existence

A named entity is an **opaque referent** (a placeholder whose existence is not implied by its appearance in the model). `exists(X)` is an explicit existential assertion *within the current worldview scope*. Thus:

```text
within scenario_modality:
    possible(exists(unrealized_realm))
    and not(exists(unrealized_realm))
```

This is coherent: the realm is said to be possible but not actual. It is neither an empirical assertion about our universe nor a logical contradiction.

**Unstated propositions remain unknown, not false.** Only explicit `not(...)` represents a denial. This is essential for representing incompletely described esoteric systems.

## 3. A worked translation: anger, reflection, and restraint

Use the same concrete scenario for all competing theories: a person feels angry, considers attacking or refraining, and refrains.

### 3.1 Ordinary descriptive structure

```text
experiences(person, anger_episode)
aware_of(person, anger_episode)
evaluates(person, attack_option)
evaluates(person, refrain_option)
selects(person, refrain_option)
performs(person, restraint_action)
realizes_option(restraint_action, refrain_option)
```

The first two lines can be present together, separately, or in a lore-defined necessary relation. That is a philosophical dispute for individual models to express—not something for our syntax to resolve.

### 3.2 Same action, competing accounts

| Model | What it additionally says | What it **does not** follow from |
|---|---|---|
| **Mechanistic reaction** | A trigger causes a behavioral outcome; Gurdjieff's ordinary human lacks unified will | A person having experiences does not establish freedom |
| **Dennettian Popperian agency** | Alternatives can be internally envisaged and assessed | Internal simulation does not show that either outcome was metaphysically realizable |
| **Compatibilism** | The agent can respond to reasons and may be morally accountable despite determinism | Accountability does not automatically require a different possible outcome |
| **Libertarian account** | The agent is able to do otherwise under relevant circumstances | Mere selection or imagination does not establish alternative possibilities |
| **Moral norm** | The agent *ought* to perform some action | An obligation does not causally compel performance |

The actual corpus records these as distinct, scoped models `G05`, `D02`, and `T01`–`T04`, `T07`. Different models may disagree without the interlingua contradicting itself.

**A caution about Dennett:** his **physical/design/intentional stances**, **Darwinian/Skinnerian/Popperian/Gregorian tower**, and **conditions of moral personhood** are separate analytical devices. We do **not** combine them into one universal ladder or assume his moral personhood conditions are both necessary and sufficient. [See Dennett source and comparative scholarship](https://philarchive.org/archive/DENCOP); [tower discussion](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8666905/).

## 5. From semantic statements to visual composition

The visual renderer should not be asked to show “consciousness” as a single primitive. It should be asked to show **specific relationships**:

| Semantic operation | Candidate visual construction | Potential misleading inference |
|---|---|---|
| Distinguishing entities | Separate, possibly iconic forms | Physical bodies assumed where none are posited |
| Awareness-of | Orient a subject's directed perspective toward a target | Attention falsely equated with experience |
| Self-awareness | The subject's perspective refers to the subject itself | A literal smaller person inside a head |
| Experiential episode | Local subjective field associated with a subject | Experience reduced to a transferable substance |
| Deliberation | Multiple represented futures inspected without executing them | Imagined choices presented as genuinely available futures |
| Actual choice | One depicted branch becomes actual | Actualization falsely interpreted as indeterminism |
| Moral norm | A conventional or compositional marker of evaluative constraint | Norm falsely appears as a physical barrier |
| Existential grounding | Stable non-transfer support relation | A packet leaving its source or occurring in time |
| Physical/cosmic transfer | Explicit carrier crossing a boundary | Any influence assumed to transport stuff |
| Logical implication | Conditional relation between whole propositions | Cause-and-effect inferred where only an entailment is stated |

**Sign modes allowed:** iconic (depicted thing resembles its referent), indexical (a sign connected to what it indicates), and conventional/symbolic (learned conventions). A small symbol vocabulary is permitted where geometry would be cryptic. No demand for a wholly non-arbitrary visual code.

**Timing rule:** animation *reveal time* is different from an event's time in the depicted cosmos. Plotinian emanation can be revealed one step at a time without claiming that the One literally generated Intellect at a clock-time.

## 6. Machine validation performed

The corpus was checked against an accompanying **JSON Schema** (rules for the permitted structure), plus an independent semantic checker for:

- Every referenced variable is declared.
- Predicate roles are complete and correctly typed.
- Modal operators use permissible domain/mode pairs (e.g., `deontic + obligatory`, not `deontic + necessary`).
- Agential `can` names an agent.
- Source identifiers exist and source-paraphrase entries have sources.
- Record identifiers are unique.
- A deliberately malformed modal expression fails as expected.

**Result: 29 records validated; 0 structural/type errors.** This verifies structural *well-formedness* (conformity to the grammar), not the philosophical accuracy, logical satisfiability, or adequacy of each paraphrase.

## 7. Known limitations and design debt

1. **Not minimal.** Fifteen grammatical sorts and 39 predicates are an exploratory vocabulary; some will likely be derived, merged, or revised. They are not 38 proposed visual primitives.
2. **No formal axiomatization yet.** The grammar currently checks whether expressions are well-formed, not whether all implications are logically provable or claims consistent within each lore. Background conditions for ability and time need stronger semantics.
3. **Translation errors remain possible.** Especially contested concepts—Plotinian self-knowledge, Bailey's soul, and Gurdjieff's will—must be checked against their passages by a human reader.
4. **Several abstract distinctions are under-specified.** An experiencing subject and an episode can be represented separately, but the ontology does not decide whether experience is ontologically independent or merely a feature of a subject. This is deliberate.
5. **No visual testing yet.** We have not established whether strangers can infer the intended structure from the eventual animation.
6. **A story's own terms still matter.** The source lexical label and locator must remain recoverable; translating into the interlingua must not erase distinctions a tradition considers central.

## 8. Historical next-step proposal (superseded)

This sequence was proposed in v0.2 and is retained to document the original research trajectory. It was not established as a gate on whether visual metaphysical thinking is possible or worthwhile. Current priorities are in [Project Status](PROJECT_STATUS.md).

Build three **short scenes** using one renderer and a shared visual grammar:

1. **Awareness vs. experience:** The same angry subject experiences an episode, becomes aware of it, then reflects on it.
2. **Deliberation vs. metaphysical ability:** The same person internally models attack and restraint; contrast deterministic and libertarian accounts without changing the observed action.
3. **Cosmic dependence vs. nourishment:** Contrast Plotinus's `ground` with Gurdjieff's `transfer` without using identical particle-flow animations.

Before expanding the language, ask viewers **what moves, what changes, what is merely represented, and what they infer is possible or necessary**. The acceptance criterion is not aesthetic appeal. It is that the visual model communicates the distinctions it was designed to preserve.
