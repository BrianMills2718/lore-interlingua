# Comparative Cosmology: Conceptual Interlingua v0.1

> **Historical/provisional record.** This file retains earlier examples and proposed next steps; current project decisions are in [Project Charter](PROJECT_CHARTER.md), [Current State](PROJECT_STATUS.md), and [Experiment Registry](EXPERIMENT_REGISTRY.md). Do not treat a past "gate" as a program-level stop rule.


**Status:** Working specification; requires scholarly review and visual comprehension testing.

**Goal:** Translate claims from distinct esoteric/philosophical traditions into a compact, compositional, source-independent conceptual representation before animation.

## Layers

1. **Source claim:** a proposition and a bibliographic locator.
2. **Interlingua:** referents, typed operators, role bindings and necessary qualifiers.
3. **Visual grammar:** rendering of operators using structure, motion and appearance, without arbitrary one-icon-per-term conventions.

## Operator vocabulary

| Operator | Semantics | Rendering strategy |
|---|---|---|
| `rank` | ordered levels on a stated comparison dimension | separate ordered strata with dimension displayed; do not infer spatial containment |
| `mediate` | a source affects a destination through an intermediary | two causally directed links through explicit intermediate locus |
| `differentiate` | one basis takes distinguishable forms; no presumption of physical loss | fan-out showing distinguishable branches, without shrinking the parent |
| `transfer` | a specified carrier is said to pass between source and recipient | one or more crossing tokens or stream only where a carrier is asserted |
| `depend_on` | one thing requires another for a particular function | sustained support link; if no carrier supplied, no particles |
| `partition` | an exhaustively grouped set, without causal implications | brackets or grouping boundaries; no motion required |
| `qualify` | an influence alters or determines a bearer’s qualities | change in a bearer’s texture or internal pattern; hue identifies variant but has no metaphysical meaning |
| `change` | a described state transition | before/after states with a timed morph only for world-temporal changes |
| `ground` | one item depends on another for its existence; not equivalent to transfer | static support relationship with stable source; never a particle stream |
| `orient` | directed attention, aspiration, or reference; not spatial movement | reorientation of a locus/axis of attention, not displacement of object |

## Translation corpus (12 paraphrased, source-grounded claims)

### Gurdjieff (reported by Ouspensky)

**G01: The Ray of Creation orders worlds from the Absolute to the Moon, with increasingly numerous governing laws.**

Source: G-O; chapters IV–V (Ray of Creation).

Operators: `rank`.

Visual test: seven ordered strata; number/constraint increases toward Moon.

Failure guard: Do not equate higher position with larger physical size. The integers refer to claimed orders of laws, not empirically observed counts.

**G02: Terrestrial organic life mediates planetary influences to the Earth.**

Source: G-O; chapter 7 (organic life as transmitting station).

Operators: `mediate`.

Visual test: planetary field -> terrestrial life -> Earth.

Failure guard: Do not assume particles or physical stuff is transported.

**G03: Organic life emits differentiated radiations toward Sun, planets, and Moon; their requirements differ.**

Source: G-O; chapter 7.

Operators: `differentiate, transfer, transfer, transfer`.

Visual test: three destination-specific outbound transmissions; not one uniform ray.

Failure guard: Represent radiations as text-specified carriers, not as known physical radiation.

**G04: The Moon is maintained by energies associated with terrestrial organic life.**

Source: G-O; chapters III and V (lunar nourishment).

Operators: `depend_on, transfer`.

Visual test: Moon retains state through a sustained incoming channel.

Failure guard: Does not imply Gurdjieff described a vampiric humanoid spirit.

### Alice Bailey

**B01: One divine Life is described as expressing itself through seven rays or builders.**

Source: B-R; The Seven Rays Enumerated, pp. 22–26.

Operators: `differentiate`.

Visual test: one source with seven branches whose distinct qualities are retained.

Failure guard: Do not show seven units of matter detached from a depleted source. The rays are also personified as Builders; do not force a single interpretation.

**B02: The seven rays are categorized as three Rays of Aspect and four Rays of Attribute.**

Source: B-Q; The Rays and Life-Quality-Appearance, pp. 158–162.

Operators: `partition`.

Visual test: a seven-element set partitioned into 3 + 4 without reducing to two rays.

Failure guard: Do not interpret the two groups as mutually hostile.

**B03: Rays condition the qualities manifested by forms throughout kingdoms of nature.**

Source: B-Q; The Rays and Life-Quality-Appearance, pp. 157–159.

Operators: `qualify`.

Visual test: a form changes a structured expressive property under ray influence.

Failure guard: An effect on the bearer is not necessarily material transferred from the ray.

**B04: A person’s soul ray and personality ray may differ and jointly shape expression, with soul ray potentially becoming dominant.**

Source: B-P; Question 4, p. 129.

Operators: `qualify, qualify, change`.

Visual test: two co-present influences modulate one person, then shift relative salience.

Failure guard: Do not imply every individual has the same rays or stages.

### Plotinus

**P01: Intellect (Nous) depends for its existence on the One; this is not an event occurring at a particular time.**

Source: P-E, P-S; Enneads V.1.6–7; SEP §2.

Operators: `ground`.

Visual test: stable grounding connection, no departing particles and no chronological birth.

Failure guard: Do not render a physical flow or loss of material by the One.

**P02: Soul is grounded in Intellect, which remains itself rather than dividing off a piece.**

Source: P-E; Enneads V.1.3.

Operators: `ground`.

Visual test: stable secondary grounding link with unchanged Intellect.

Failure guard: No detachment, depletion, or literal physical descent.

**P03: Soul gives life to the sensible cosmos without being partitioned into physical pieces.**

Source: P-E; Enneads V.1.2.

Operators: `qualify, ground`.

Visual test: many living things participate in one undivided source.

Failure guard: Do not depict Soul as a stock of energy consumed by bodies.

**P04: The soul turns its contemplation toward Intellect as its source and means of perfection.**

Source: P-E; Enneads V.1.3.

Operators: `orient`.

Visual test: alignment of attention toward Intellect, not physical upward travel.

Failure guard: Do not turn the orientation of thought into literal travel through space.

## Concrete semantic differences to preserve

| Pair | Shared structure | Non-equivalence |
|---|---|---|
| G03 / B01 | differentiation and directed expression | G03 posits destination-specific radiations; B01 posits seven divine rays/builders; not equivalent physical carriers |
| B01 / P01 | a dependent plurality or subordinate principle with a supreme source | Bailey has seven differentiations; Plotinus asserts atemporal grounding of Intellect and denies loss from the One |
| G04 / P02 | one term structurally depends on another | G04 is functional nourishment using a carrier; P02 is existence-grounding without carrier or depletion |
| B03 / P03 | a higher principle bears on the characteristics of lower forms | B03 confers differentiated ray qualities; P03 connects the presence of life to indivisible Soul |

## Failure tests

1. **Reject:** P01 shown with glowing particles leaving the One. **Reason:** imports time and physical transfer into atemporal grounding.
2. **Reject:** B01 seven rays shown as seven chunks removed from one source. **Reason:** implies depletion unsupported by the mapped claim.
3. **Reject:** G03 and B01 collapsed because both use rays/radiations. **Reason:** lexical similarity is not operator equivalence.
4. **Reject:** All upward/descending orientation treated as literal location. **Reason:** hierarchy, valuation, causation and spatial position differ.

## Invariants

- Identical words never imply equivalent concepts.
- Different words never imply different concepts without relational analysis.
- Atemporal grounding must not render as chronological creation.
- The visual representation may not add unasserted material transfer.
- Animation used for viewer reveal must be distinguishable from events within a cosmology.
- Missing qualifiers remain unknown, not silently filled by the renderer.
- Colors may distinguish variants but must not encode intrinsic moral status by default.

## Historical pre-implementation proposal (superseded)

This is the original v0.1 recommendation. It is not the current project plan or a project-level performance gate.


Produce three pilot scenes (lunar nourishment, Bailey’s ray differentiation, Plotinus’s atemporal grounding). Ask viewers to describe the represented relationship, its direction, whether something is transferred, and whether it occurs in time. Do not generalize the primitives until the misunderstandings have been documented.
