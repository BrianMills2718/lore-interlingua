## Proposed experiment (not yet on `main`)

**014 — Conceptual representation before storyboard:** [PR #37](https://github.com/BrianMills2718/lore-interlingua/pull/37) proposes a source-scoped G02 model, five provisional notation mappings and two alternative explanatory traversals. It is **unmerged as of this index update**. It contains neither a storyboard nor a rendered film, has not been studied with viewers, and should not be counted as a completed result until its PR merges.

**Cross-experiment result:** No proposed general visual-grammar rule has been promoted as independently established, and no finished film was generated automatically from the shared interlingua. These are **research and artistic prototypes**, not independently validated milestones toward a verified visual compiler.

## Main questions actually learned

- **Selection:** We must choose the meaningful processes and beings *inside* a lore before making generic scenery or comparison charts.
- **Casting:** Unnamed circles and figures invite unintended claims (e.g., interpreting created entities as the Trinity).
- **Literalism:** A physical metaphor can be excellent without its physical substrate being part of the ontology; the right test concerns its dominant correspondences and distortions.
- **Motion:** Replacing one proposition card with the next is presentation animation, not necessarily dynamic reasoning.
- **Time:** Distinguish world time, reveal time, and manipulation time. Timeless concepts have long been visually representable; our challenge is expressive and compositional.
- **Translation:** v0.2 predicates and ad hoc scene manifests are not yet a usable semantic interchange contract.

## Classification conventions for future work

Every new experiment should declare **purpose** (research exploration, grammar test, or public lore film), **lore scope and source**, **representation and manipulation affordances**, **intended metaphorical correspondences**, **known distortions**, **rendering source dependencies**, and **provisional/accepted status**. The form is lightweight; no ceremony should displace artistic iteration.

Avoid interpreting a plan within an earlier experiment as a current project directive. Use [PROJECT_STATUS](PROJECT_STATUS.md) for current priorities and [PROJECT_CHARTER](PROJECT_CHARTER.md) for governing principles.
