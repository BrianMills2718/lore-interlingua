What the current condition illustrates

Two colored influence families are invented examples of different kinds, not source-defined planetary energies. Bare ground is a research counterfactual, not a claim that Earth was historically bare. Every celestial and living object stays in the same spatial setting while its represented condition is varied.

What the source actually says

Organic life allows planetary influences to reach Earth. A grass-covered field and the same field occupied by people receive and transmit influences of different kinds. The text does not identify their colors, motion laws, or quantitative differences. Read Ouspensky excerpts.

What this image does not assert

No atmospheric particle physics, cosmic receptor chemistry, causal equation, exact number of channels, or prediction about human groups. The two illustrative channels are an artistic way to express selective reception and the mediation relationship—not a scientific model.
