# Experiment 010 — The Living Intermediary

**Status:** interactive artistic/semantic research study, not a finished lore film, astronomical simulation, or established visual grammar. Open [index.html](index.html) offline in a browser. No audio, online resources, or dependencies are needed to use the scene.

## The source problem

P. D. Ouspensky's record of Gurdjieff describes organic life as Earth's sensitive intermediary for influences from the planetary sphere. The text says those influences would not otherwise reach Earth and gives a concrete contrast: the same field receives and transmits different influences when covered in grass versus occupied by a crowd. See [quotations attributed to Ouspensky](https://www.gurdjieff-hawaii.org/?page_id=160) and *In Search of the Miraculous*, the organic-life discussion, commonly in chapter 7 (locators vary by edition).

The source supports **mediation** and **different reception by different living configurations**. It does **not** specify the number of distinct planetary influences, colors, frequencies, biological transduction mechanisms, measured rates, or a literal physics of cosmic particles.

## One scene, three exploratory conditions

- **Bare ground:** an explicit *counterfactual research control*. The image shows incoming influences without a living intermediary, so no depicted transmission into Earth occurs. This is not a historical assertion about a lifeless period of the planet or a universal claim that ordinary physical forces do not act on bare ground.
- **Grassland:** visible blades participate in receiving **illustrative family A** (cyan moving points), followed by a brief luminous response entering Earth. Other possible influences remain faint.
- **Human gathering:** the *same terrain* is occupied by recognizable people who receive **illustrative family B** (violet rings) and show a correspondingly distinct surface/Earth response.

The two families A and B are **invented graphic placeholders** for the source's unspecified distinction, not a named two-class cosmic taxonomy. Choosing a condition is an exploratory change in the depicted *instance*, not a timeline or replacement cosmology. The same celestial background, terrestrial ground, and camera remain in view.

## Core visual operations

1. **Reception:** an incoming mark terminates at a living subject, rather than at an anonymous surface.
2. **Participation:** the living subject reacts locally; the subject isn't just a conduit drawn between endpoints.
3. **Mediation to Earth:** a new, short-lived luminous response enters the Earth region after reception and is absent in the bare counterfactual. No persistent physical pipes or misleading structural trajectory lines.
4. **Differentiation:** grass and people select **different illustration types** from the *same* pool of symbolic influence possibilities. The difference in kind is source-grounded; the precise graphic-to-kind assignment is not.

The interface supports mode changes, play/pause, timeline scrubbing, hiding non-received marks, and research-only diagnostic annotation. It does not invite a viewer to infer an unsourced physiological or quantitative filtering model.

## Visual parsimony audit

| Prominent feature | Why it exists | Not a claim about |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Three celestial disks | Recognizable plural source class | Three actual named planets or exactly three kinds |
| Same layered Earth | Distinguish recipient from organic mediator; keep geography fixed | Geological or spiritual planes |
| Grass blades / people | Two living configurations named in Ouspensky's example | Physiological mechanism of cosmic reception |
| Cyan filled marks / violet rings | Two *illustrative* kinds of incoming relation | Named wavelengths, energy species or frequencies |
| Surface response and subsurface pulse | Make the role attributed to the intermediary visibly consequential | Literal measured forces, energy conservation or a physical field equation |
| Faint non-received marks | Demonstrate selective visual reception rather than ambient clutter | Literal rejection or reflection of particles |
| Stars, terrain texture | Orientation and artistic depth (nonsemantic) | A source-defined hierarchy |

**Research question:** Does a visible local response clarify *mediation*, or encourage an unsupported physical-filtering metaphor? This is a productive design question, not an automatic reason to eliminate metaphor.
