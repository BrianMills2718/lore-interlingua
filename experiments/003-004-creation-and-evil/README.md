# Experiments 003–004: Manichaeism and Catholicism

**Status:** Two completed 28-second audiovisual-free programmatic animations; preliminary semantic comparison. Each has a source-linked conceptual model, tested renderer, and five-frame storyboard in the complete experiment bundle supplied with the conversation.

## Aim
Compare how the traditions represent creation, evil, embodiment, and final salvation **without** conflating similar visual metaphors.

- **Manichaeism:** two independent primordial realms, Light and Darkness; divine light trapped in matter; cosmic apparatus including Sun and Moon acts to free captive light; eventual separation.
- **Catholicism:** one uncreated God freely creates an ordered and good world *ex nihilo*; evil is not a coeternal independent deity; redemption through Christ and renewal of creation including embodied life.

This is an internal-lore comparison, **not** an endorsement of either claim as physical cosmology.
