## What a viewer is now told—and what remains unknown

1. **What is the Ray?** One selected hierarchy of worlds; the persistent highlighted relation is not a beam that carries cosmic particles. The camera's movement is *presentation*, not a historical sequence of the world's creation.
2. **What are the laws?** In Ouspensky's report, the numbers indicate *orders of laws or governing forces*. More orders mean greater mechanicalness in lower worlds. The animation's repetitive geometry is a *selective analogy*, not a drawing of 48 or 96 separate rules. The film does not enumerate the individual laws.
3. **What is the Law of Three?** Active, passive, and neutralizing forces are *roles* that meet in phenomena. The meeting graphic is schematic. Their permanent colored identities or cosmic mechanism are **not established**.
4. **What is the Law of Seven?** The account explains developmental processes as octaves requiring additional shocks at intervals. The chosen cosmic example has an interval from planetary world to Earth; organic life fills it. The film does not teach the complete doctrine of musical octaves or claim literal astronomical sound waves.
5. **What arrives?** The source calls these *planetary influences*. Their modern physical substance, trajectories, speeds and exact mechanism are **not provided by the cited passage**. Luminous marks are labels-in-motion, not sampled physical particles.
6. **What does life do?** Plants, animals and people make the intermediary visible. Incoming marks meet the living surface and separately originated inward pulses depict Earth receiving influences *through* life. Local colors and pulses are art-directed, not a physiological converter model.
7. **What goes outward?** The source distinguishes radiation to Sun, planets, and Moon. The three glyphs are *conventional categories* paired with different recipients; different colors do not establish independent radiation mechanisms. The Moon's nourishment remains a **stronger contextual claim** not explained by merely depicting reception.

## Unresolved artistic and semantic issues

- Does the short Law-of-Three interaction meaningfully teach a *three-role relation* or merely create an attractive diagram? A longer voiceover could distinguish role changes without inventing three physical substances.
- Does the structural Ray read as a selected hierarchical relation rather than a cosmic conduit? Future composition could make nesting and the larger unselected plurality clearer.
- Are grasses, trees, an animal and humans recognizably *different ways of receiving* without over-specifying cosmic biophysics? This distinction remains mostly carried by labels and selective color coding.
- Does the Law-of-Seven interval feel like a **process interruption in the cosmic octave**, not an empty distance between two planets? The film labels it but does not depict the entire octave theory.
- No empirical test of unfamiliar-viewer comprehension or derivation of these graphics from a generic interlingua has been performed.

**Audio:** the score is project-authored and has no narration. The SRT is present and the English explanations are already burned in. The film is designed so high-quality narration can be recorded later without rebuilding the concept sequence.
