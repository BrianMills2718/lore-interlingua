# Natural-voice narration draft — The Living Interval

**Status (2026-10-29):** superseded for the narrated preview by the story-told [narration.json](narration.json), recorded with Edge TTS by `narrate.py`; kept as the source-scoped paraphrase. **Original status:** unrecorded draft aligned with the finished 176-second picture. The MP4 intentionally has ambient music and burned-in explanatory captions, with no robotic synthesized speech. This draft can support a later human recording. It is a paraphrase of Gurdjieff's teaching as recorded by P. D. Ouspensky, **not a quotation from Ouspensky**. Time ranges follow the baked-in caption cue structure; a narrator would rehearse pacing and edit rather than mechanically force each paragraph into its interval.

| 02:50–02:56 | The hierarchy and living world are one connected account. |

## Editorial caveats before recording

- Do not imply the script is a primary-source quotation, a verified astronomical theory, or a laboratory measurement of cosmic influence.
- Distinguish the Law of Three (relational roles) from the cumulative count of law orders; the film does not enumerate the orders or demonstrate a literal mathematical mechanism for each.
- Distinguish the Law of Seven's interval from empty astronomical distance or sound emitted by celestial bodies.
- The outgoing radiation and Moon nourishment are separate claims; the film does not demonstrate the Moon's maintenance.
- Source scope: P. D. Ouspensky, *In Search of the Miraculous*, relevant cosmology and organic-life passages; local corpus G01, G02, G03, with G04 contextual.
