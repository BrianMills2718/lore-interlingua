The living relation.

Interpretation, not physics. The source describes the relationship. The diagram does not assert particles, measurable energy, speeds, or a biological conversion mechanism.

    06 / WHAT THIS DOES NOT PROVE

    Implementation is not comprehension.

    To test understanding, ask an unfamiliar viewer to reconstruct the source → intermediary → recipient relation and distinguish reception from mediation. To test notation reuse, teach the signs and apply them to a separately justified second example. Neither study has been conducted here.
