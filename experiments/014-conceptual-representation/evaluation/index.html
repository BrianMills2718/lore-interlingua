FORMATIVE VIEWER STUDY · VERSION 1

What did you understand?

This exercise asks whether a conceptual explanation was clear and whether two proposed visual conventions make sense in another context. The question is about understanding the account, not believing it.

STAGE 2 / SOURCE-CASE UNDERSTANDING

Explain the relationship

Answer from the explanation you just viewed. You may select “Not sure.” Avoid returning to the teaching tab while answering, so we can see what remained understandable.

C1. Which description matches the source-scoped relationship presented?
C2. In this *specific represented relationship*, what is implied if the intermediary is absent?
C3. What physical information is established by the dashed and solid marks?

Your responses are not scored or displayed as right/wrong here. They will be locked when you continue.

STAGE 3 / NEW CONTEXT

Can you recognize the notation?

This is a hypothetical communication situation, not a Gurdjieff teaching. The three participants have arbitrary names. Their roles are intentionally not supplied: use the marks learned in the earlier example. The arrangement is reversed from the previous diagram.

T1. Which connection represents reception in the notation you learned?
T2. Which connection represents mediation in that notation?
T3. Using only the diagram, who is in the intermediary role?
T4. What can you infer about the mechanism from this diagram alone?
