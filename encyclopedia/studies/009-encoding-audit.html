When three symbols are not three mechanisms.

Gurdjieff's organic-life account gives different cosmic recipients different kinds of radiations. Our animation depicts three colors, shapes and pathways. Does this show three mechanisms, or only three categories aimed at different destinations?

Manipulate the visible code

All three modes preserve the underlying G03 statement. Only the expressive mapping changes. Movement is diagram time, not measured cosmic activity.

0:04

The optional G02 context shows reception (cyan) and onward terrestrial mediation (green). G03 consists of the three outgoing families. All colors and shapes are artistic codes, not particle identities.

What the intervention reveals

Remains invariant

Organic life is the radiating subject; Sun, planetary world, and Moon are distinct recipients. G03 asserts that the required kinds differ.

What changes

What we cannot infer

Different glyphs, rhythms or colors do not establish three different physical mechanisms, receiver behaviors, or cosmological frequencies.

Original implementation

Three outgoing streams share the same marker-on-a-curve procedure, with different shapes, colors, destinations and arbitrary rates: 0.095, 0.067, 0.083 path fractions per second.

Source boundary

G02 = planetary reception and terrestrial mediation. G03 = outgoing differentiated radiation. G04 = lunar nourishment, not demonstrated by arrival alone.

Recommended artistic response

  • Keep distinctive marks when communicating the source's different kinds; do not make them falsely scientific.
  • Standardize arbitrary rate differences unless intentionally exploring presentation rhythm.
  • Develop G02's local organic reception: the source explicitly contrasts a field of grass and a human gathering as different receivers.
  • Do not invent recipient-specific physical reactions for G03 simply to make the animation look mechanistic.
  • Use source-scoped text and optional research keys; keep the film free of an intrusive permanent legend.
Sources, distinctions and limits

The research result is a semantic and implementation audit. It does not demonstrate how a new viewer will decode the marks. The uniform and permuted versions are internal counterfactual changes to the drawing, not alternate Gurdjieff doctrines.
