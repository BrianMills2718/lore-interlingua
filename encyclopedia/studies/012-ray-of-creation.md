# Experiment 012 — A visual grammar of selected worlds and increasing constraints

**Status:** provisional visual-language research derived from an audience-facing continuous Gurdjieff film, [*The Ray of Creation*](../../experiments/012-ray-of-creation/README.md). This is an encyclopedia **case-study record**, not an assertion of an ontology of objective physical reality, and not a generic animation compiler.

## Source-scoped propositions

The established interlingua corpus already contains the Gurdjieff Ray of Creation as claim **G01**. In P. D. Ouspensky's account of Gurdjieff's teaching, a sequence of seven worlds is distinguished by progressively larger **orders of laws**: Absolute 1; All Worlds 3; All Suns (a chosen galaxy in the selected chain) 6; Sun 12; planetary world 24; Earth 48; Moon 96. See [*In Search of the Miraculous*](https://www.globalgreyebooks.com/online-ebooks/p-d-ouspensky_in-search-of-the-miraculous_complete-text.html). Higher worlds are characterized as closer to the will of the Absolute; lower worlds as increasingly mechanical. These numbers and qualitative distinctions are *source propositions*, not astronomical quantities independently established by the film.

## Reusable candidate relations

| Relation | Design treatment | Legitimate implication | Misleading inference to reject |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Selected hierarchy** | One persistent highlighted strand through seven distinguishable worlds; faint unselected branches | One sequence is selected from broader levels of worlds | Energy physically travels down a glowing cable; other branches are eight new levels |
| **Nested or originated within** | Continuous scale and visual casting maintain the hierarchy of source scopes | Viewer can hold local and global structure in the same composition | Screen distance equals cosmic distance; the camera records an actual birth chronology |
| **Increasing constraint** | A recognizable motion motif becomes increasingly recurrent, overlapped and spatially restricted; exact law counts supplied by text | An artistic rendering of the qualitative idea of greater mechanicalness | Each moving curve is one objectively identified Gurdjieff law; 96 laws means 96 hoops |
| **Identity across scales** | Distinct iconic Sun, chosen galactic world, planets, living Earth and Moon persist through camera travel | Named referents are recognizable and not interchangeable tokens | A planet glyph gives the scale, size or morphology of astronomy |
| **Presentation time ≠ world time** | Camera traverses a complete extant conceptual model, then reveals a full view | A sequence can make an atemporal ordering comprehensible | The Absolute created the Moon in two minutes |

The film's visual language differs deliberately from Experiment 011's moving **influence** marks. In 011, motes represent directed receptions and radiations. In 012, the selected gold-white strand represents the **model's genealogical structure**; it is not an influence stream. This typed distinction should survive future compositions.

## Dependency on existing ontology

Navigation concepts: **unity**, **plurality**, **part–whole**, **dependence**, **process**, **time**, **mediation** and **identity**. But no existing navigation category alone adequately describes *a selected hierarchy in a doctrine-specific succession of worlds*, so the study records it as a typed relation hypothesis without automatically declaring another universal concept.

## Limitations and follow-up

This is an art-directed realization, not a formally verified visual syntax. The changing path motif may read as accumulating decoration rather than increased constraint; the closing wide view is necessarily tiny for all seven levels. Those are artistic and semantic questions to study in future films. Do not use a numerical pass/fail gate to invalidate the project; examine the specific expressive problems and correct them.

**What should be retained:** the central habit of accounting for every major mark, keeping motion semantics separate from decorative motion, source-scoping claims, and preserving an unbroken spatial composition while changing conceptual focus.
