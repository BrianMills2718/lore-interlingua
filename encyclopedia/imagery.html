THE MISSING BRIDGE

The planetary–Earth interval

In Ouspensky's report of Gurdjieff, the Law of Seven describes a cosmic octave with an interval between the planetary world and Earth. Organic life fills that interval: without it Earth would not receive those planetary influences. That joins the seven-level hierarchy from Film 012 to the living intermediary in Film 011. A future film should show this connection, not just play the two videos consecutively.

Selected-world hierarchy, incoming planetary influences, and outgoing radiation are three different relations. We must not represent all three as interchangeable beams.

SOURCES AND BOUNDARIES

The named relations are historical esoteric claims in Gurdjieff's teaching as recorded by P. D. Ouspensky. They are not claimed to be verified astrophysics. Colors, repeated rhythms, beams, and camera geometry are artistic interpretations. Source claims, inferred relations and speculative mechanisms remain distinct.
